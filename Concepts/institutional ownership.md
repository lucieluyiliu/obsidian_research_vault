The fraction of a firm's shares held by institutional investors (mutual funds, pension funds, insurance companies, etc.). Commonly decomposed into domestic vs. foreign (`IODOM`/`IOFOR`), active vs. passive, and by investor type (Big 3, hedge funds, etc.).
